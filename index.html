About

The RUET Course Registration Form Generator is a specialized web application designed specifically for students of Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET) to generate their course registration forms in the official university format.

The tool automates the process of creating registration forms, ensuring compliance with RUET's official formatting requirements while providing an intuitive and user-friendly interface.
